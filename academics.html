ACADEMICS

The PSHS 6-year Curriculum Framework describes what scholars can become as they progress through the six years of STEM[1]-based high school education, which is divided into Foundation Years, Advancement Years, and Specialization Years. It incorporates essential knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes that scholars should be equipped with in preparation to become STEM practitioners and leaders in the future. At the end of the 6-year program, scholars are expected to be skilled in STEM, humanistic in spirit, global in perspective, and patriotic in orientation, and to carry the values of integrity, excellence, and service to the nation.

In the process of learning, scholars should be able to integrate and interconnect essential knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes to develop general capabilities. The PSHS?s general capabilities are designed to be relevant to the framework of the 21st century and beyond, and they aim to empower scholars to thrive as future-proof learners. Milligan and co-researchers (2020) define future proofing as the acquisition of vital skills that will better-prepare students to efficiently navigate and thrive in the ever-evolving workplace environment. They also refer to these skills as ?21st-century skills, general capabilities, graduate attributes or transversal skills? (Milligan et al., 2020, p.3).

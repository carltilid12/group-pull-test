Young Minds, Big Ideas: Pisay Scholars Pitch the Future at KIC 2026

The DOST–Philippine Science High School (PSHS) System brought together young innovators from its campuses nationwide for the Kids' Innovation Camp (KIC) 2026, held on November 4–6, 2026, at Axiaa Hotel in Quezon City.

Organized through the PSHS System's Knowledge and Innovation Division (KID), KIC is a system-wide initiative that connects Pisay scholars with Research and Development Institutions (RDIs) nationwide, providing opportunities to transform promising research ideas into innovations that address community needs and national development priorities.

During the three-day gathering, student teams showcased their proposed research projects through a Community Fair and Startup Pitch Sessions, presenting their ideas before experts from various DOST agencies and other private research institutes. The activities challenged scholars to demonstrate the originality, technical feasibility, and potential impact of their proposed innovations.

Outstanding entries were also recognized through the Best Poster and Best Pitching Awards.

But the innovation journey does not end with the competition. KIC forms part of a structured innovation pathway for scholars from Grade 10 to Grade 12.

Through continued collaboration with RDIs, participating students are expected to receive technical guidance and mentorship as they refine their research, explore intellectual property protection, and work toward developing functional prototypes and/or patent applications.

These efforts will eventually culminate in the presentation of their research outputs at the Science Research Summit next year.
